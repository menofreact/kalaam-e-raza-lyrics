Mere Aqa Aoo Ke Muddat Hoi hai, Teri Rah Main Akhiyan Bichchate Bichchate

Teri Hasraton Main,Teri Chahton Main Bade din Howe Ghar Sajaate Sajaate

Mera Yeh hai Emaan,Yeh Mera Yakeen Hai -Mere Mustafa Sa Na Koi Haseen Hai

Ke Rukh Unka Dekha Hai Jabse Qamar Ne Nikalta Hai Moo Ko Chupate Chupate

Qayamat Ka Manzar Bada Purkhatar Hai – Magar Mustafa ka Jo Deewana Ho Ga

Who Pul Pay se Guzre Ga Masroor Ho Ke – Naara Nabi Ka Lagate Lagate

Mere Lab Pe Mola,Na Koi Sada Hai – Fakat Mujh Nikamme ki Yeh Hi Dua Hai

Meri Saans Nikle Dar-e- Musftafa Pe – Gham-e-Dil Nabi Ko Sunate Sunate

Yeh Mana ke Ek Din Aani Kaza Hai – Magar Dosto Tumse Yeh iltaja Hai

Ke Shehr-e-Muhammad Ki Har Ek Gali Se – Jaza Uthana Ghumaate Ghumaate

Na Kaabe Se Matlab Na Masjid ki Chahat – Fakat Dil Main Haakim Tamanna Yahi Jo Mil Jaaye Naksh-e-Kafay Paaye Ahmed -Tu Mar Jaayen Sir ko Jhukate Jhukate
