Maqbool Dua Karna Manzoor Sana Karna

Mid-hat Ka Sila Dena Maqbool Sana Karna

Dil Zikre Shareef Unka Har Subho Masa Karna

Din Raat Japa Karna Har Aan Rata Karna

Seene Pe Qadam Rakhna Dil Shaad Mera Karna

Dard E Dil E Muztar Ki Sarkaar Dawa Karna

Sansaar Bhikari Hai Jag Daata Daya Karna

Hai Kaam Tumhara Hi Sarkaar Ataa Karna

Kab Aapke Kunche Me Mangta Ko Sada Karna

Khud Bheek Liye Tumko Mangta Ko Nida Karna

Din Raat Hai Taibah Me Daulat Ka Luta Karna

Mangta Ki Dawa Karna Mangta Ka Bhala Karna

Din Raat Khatao Par Humko Hai Khata Karna

Aur Tumko Ataao Par Har Dam Hai Ataa Karna

Hum Apne Khatao Par Naadim Bhi Nahi Hote

Aur Unko Ataao Par Har Baar Hai Ataa Karna

WALLAH Wo Sun Lenge Aur Dil Ki Dawa Denge

Bekaar N Jayega Faryado Buka Karna

Aada Ko Khuda Waala Jab Tumne Bana Daala

Dushwar Hai Tum Par Kya Mujh Badd Ka Bhala Karna

Taibah Me Bula Lena Aur Apna Banaa Lena

Qaidi Ghum E Furqat Ke Sarkaar Rihaa Karna

Hum Arz Kiye Jaaye Sarkaar Sune Jaaye

Kya Duur Karam Se Hai Din Aisa Shaha Karna

Har Daagh Mita Dena Aur Dil Ko Shifa Dena

Aayina Bana Dena Aisi Tu Jhila Dena

Parde Me Jo Rehte Ho Parda Hai Chale Aao

Aankhon Me Basa Karna Tum Dil Me Raha Karna

Hu Tishna Magar Saqee Deedar Ke Sharbat Ka

Ik Jaam Mujhe Pyare LILLAH Ataa Karna

Hai Pyaas Se Haal Abtar Nikli Hai Zubaa(n) Baahar

Ik Jaam Mujhe Sarwar Kauthar Ka Ataa Karna

Wo Tera  Bura Chahe Mumkin Hi Nahi Un se

A’ada Ki Bhalayi Ki Jinko Hai Dua Karna

Duniya Bane Ya Bigde Duniya Rahe Ya Jaaye

Tu Deen Bana Pyare Duniya Ka Hai Kya Karna

Khaya Piya Aur Pehna Achho Se Raha Achha

Kuch Deen Ka Bhi Karle Duniya Ka Hai Kya Karna

Barbaad Na Ho Mitti IsKhaaq Ke Putle Ki

ALLAH Mujhe Unki Khaaqe Kafe Paa Karna

Kyu Naqshe Kafe Paa Ko Dil Se Na Lagaye Wo
