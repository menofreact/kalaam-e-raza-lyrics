Hum Aaimmah ke muqallid hain tamaamHai Imamaun ka hamay bhi ehteraam

Har Imam e waqt ne Deen ke liyeWaqf ki hai jaan apni la kalaam

Hai jahaan mein Hazrath Numaan bhiMazhab Hanfi ke sar-kirdah Imaam

Aapne chaha yehi aik aik palSur-khuru ho Ummate Khairul Anaam

Ijtihaad aisa kiya Ai marhabaMujtahid ho kar raha duniya me naam

Masle tishnah na chore Aap neHal kiye uljhe huwe thay jo tamaam

Waqt ke aahang ne Azam kahaAap ko to Ai fiqiyah wa Ai Imam

Faiz paya Aap se ahnaaf neWo saher ho ya ke ho wo koee shaam

Nazar karna hai yeh mukhtaar e hazeenAap ko Ai Bu Haneefa As-Salaam
