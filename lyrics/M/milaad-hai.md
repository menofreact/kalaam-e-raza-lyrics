Maah-E-Rabi-Ul-Awwal Mauqa Hai Iss Khushi Ka  Behtar Ho Iss Mein Kasrat Milaad-E-Mustafa Ki

Farsh-E-Zameen Se Leykar Arsh-E-A’zeem Tak Hai  Ye Saari Khair-O-Barkat Milaad-E-Mustafa Ki

Iblis Ke Jigar Per Chaltay Issiyare  Iss Per Giraan Hai Shirkat Milaad-E-Mustafa Ki
