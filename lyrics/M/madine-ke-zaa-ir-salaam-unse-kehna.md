Madine Ke Zaa-ir Salaam Unse Kehna

Tadapte Hai Tere Gulam Unse Kehna

Ho Jab Saamne Sabz Ghumbad tumhare

Nigahe Aqeedat Se Daaman Pasare

Hai Haazir Tumhara Ghulam Unse Kehna

Badi Chahato Se Hai Iss Darr Ko Paaya

Pada Hi Rahu Darr Na Chute Tumhara

Na Jaunga Ab Tishnakaam Unse Kehna

Phire Kab Talak Darr Ba-Darr Be Sahare

Kise Jaye Hum Apne Dil Ki Sunane

Tumhi To Banate Ho Kaam Unse Kehna

Isi Aarzu Me Guzarte Rahe Din

Ki Pahuche Dayare Nabi Hum Bhi Lekin

Nahi Hai Koi Intezam Unse Kehna

Dikha DoAnasKo Dilkash Nazare

Taraste Hai Jinko Musalmaan Saare

Ye Baatein Basad Ehteraam Unse Kehna
