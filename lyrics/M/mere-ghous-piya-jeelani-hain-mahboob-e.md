Mere Ghous Piya Jeelani Hain Mahboob E Subhaani

Choot-ti Hain To Choote Duniya, Ghous Ka Daaman Na Chorenge

Apne Gale Mein Ghous Ka Patta, Ghous Ka Daaman Na Chorenge

Ghous Ke Darr Par Umar Guzari, Ghous Ke Darr Ke Hum Hain Bhikari

Iss Khoote Se Khud Ko Baandha, Ghous Ka Daaman Na Chorenge

Waliyo Ne Di Unko Salaami, Abdalo Ne Kee Hai Ghulami

Ooncha Rahega Unka Jhanda, Ghous Ka Daaman Na Chorenge

Ghous Ka Daaman Kaise Chore, Jism O Rooh Ka Naata Unse

Unse Thehra Deen Ka Rishta, Ghous Ka Daaman Na Chorenge

Unke Haath Me Haath Diya Hain, Khud Ko UjagarBech Diya Hain

Ab Na Kabhi Chorenge WALLAH, Ghous Ka Daaman Na Chorenge

Ghous E Paak Ke Chahne Walo, Saath Ubaid Ke Mil Ke Keh Do

Marte Damm Tak In-sha-Allah, Ghous Ka Daaman Na Chorenge
