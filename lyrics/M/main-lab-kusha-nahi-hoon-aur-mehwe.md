# Main lab kusha nahi hoon aur mehwe ilteja hu

**Categories:** M, Other Naat Shareef
**Source:** https://www.kalaam-e-raza.in/main-lab-kusha-nahi-hoon-aur-mehwe/
**Lines:** 5

---

Koi to aankh waala guzrega is taraf seTaibah ke raaste me main muntazir khada hu

Ye roshni si kya hai, khushboo kahan se aayishayad main chalte chalte ROUZE tak aa gaya hu

Taibah ke sab bhikari pehchante hai mujhkomujhko khabar nahi thi main is kadar bada hu

Doori o haazri mein ek baat mushtarak haikuch khwaab dekhta tha kuch khwaab dekhta hu

Iqbaal mujhko ab bhi mehsus ho raha haike ROUZE ke saamne hu aur naat padh raha hu
