Meeran Waliyon ke Imam De Do Panjtan ke Naam

Humne Jholi Hai Phailayi Bari Dair Se

Daalo Nazr-e-Karam Sarkar,Apne Mangton Par Ek Baar

Humne Mehfil Hai Saja’yi Bari Dair Se

Mere Chand Main Sadqe Aa Ja Idher Bhi

Chamak Uththe Dilki Gali Ghoos-e-Azam

Tere Rab ne Ma’alik Kiya Tere Jadd Ko

Tere Dar se Duniya Pali Ghoos-e-Azam

Tera Rutba A’ala Na Kiun Ho Ke Maula

Tu Hai Ibn-e-Maula Ali Ghoos-e-Azam

Qadam Gardan-e-Awliya Per Hai Tera

Tu Hai Rab ka Aisa Wali Ghoos-e-Azam

Tum jo banao Baat Bane Gi,Meeran Tumjo Banao

Dono Jahan Main Laa’j Rahe Gi,La’j Rahe Gi

Lajpa’al Karam ab Kardo,Mangton ki Jhooli Bhar Do

Bhar Do Kaa’sa Sabka Panjtani Khair Se

Mushkil Jab Bhi Sir Par Aayi,Mushkil Jab Bhi Sir Par Aayi,

Teri Rehmat Aare Aayi,Teri Rehmat Aare Aayi,Aare Aayi

Jab Main ne Tumhen Pukara,Kamaya Tera Sahara
