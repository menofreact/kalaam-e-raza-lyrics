Parho Durood Kay Maulood Ki Ghari Aayi

Har Ek Saa’at O Lamha Hai Qeemti Iss KaHar Ek Raat Se Barh Kar Mila Isay RutbaIsi Liye To Ye Shab Behtareen Kehlaayi

Khuda Ka Shukr Karo Aur Parho Durood Un PerKe Aaj Laaye Hain Tashreef Haadi O RehbarZamanay Bhar Mein Hai Mash-hoor Jin Ki Yaktayi

Chhatay Hain Badal E Ghum Khushiyan Saath Laayi HainHar Ek Chehray Pe Ab Muskurahat Aayi HaiHar Ek Dil Mein Khushi Ki Baji Hai Shehnai

Jahan Mein Sabz Parchamo(n) Ki Bahaar Aayi HaiAur Aashiqo(n) Ne Makaan Har Gali Sajaai HaiHai Shaad Shaad Nabi Ka Har Ek Tamannayi

Jahan Goonj Utha Marhaba Ke Naaron SeKhushi Hai Kaisi Na Poocho Ye Ghum Ke Maaron SeYehi Wohi Raat Hai Jo Kaif Ka Samaan Layi

Unhi Ke Dar Se Hi Darya E Jood Behtay HainWo Aaye Jo Nahi Saail Ko Nahi Kehte HainSakhawat Aisi Ataa Rab Ne Un Ko Farmayi
