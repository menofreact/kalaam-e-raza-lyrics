Phir ke galli galli taybah thokare sab ki khayen kyoon

Dil ko jo akal de Khuda teri gali se jayen kyoon

Rukhsate qaafila ka shor gashse hame uthae kyoonSote hain Unke Sayen mein koyi hamme jagaaye kyoon

Yaade Huzoor ki qasam gaflate aish he sitamKhoob hai qaide ghum mein hum koyee hamme chhurae kyoon

Dekh ke Hazrate Gani phel pare Faqeer bhiChaayi he ab to chauni Hashr hi aana jae kyoon

Jaan he Ishq e Mustafa roz fizoo kare KhudaJis ko ho dard ka mazaa naaze dawa uthae kyoon

Woh jo nathe to kuch natha woh jo naho to kuch nahoJaan hai woh jahan ki, jaan hai to jahaan hai

Hai to Raza nira Sitam jurm pe gar lajaaye humKoyee bajaaye soze gam saze tarab bajaaye kyoon
