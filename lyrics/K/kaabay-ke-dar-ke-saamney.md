Ka’abay Le Dar Ke Saamney Maangi Hai Ye Dua Faqat

Haatho(n) Me Hashr Tak Rahe Daaman – E – Mustafa Faqat

Apne Karam Se Ay Khuda Ishq E Rasool Kar Ataa

Aakhri Dum Labo(n) Pe Ho Salle A’la Sadaa Faqat

Taibah Ki Sayr Ko Chale Qadmo Mein Unke Jaan De

Daffan Ho Unke Sheher Mein Hai Yahi Iltejaa Faqat

Duniya Ki Saari Nea’matein Meri Nazar Mein Hainch Ho

Rakhney Ko Sar Pay Gar Milay Na’lein E Mustafa Faqat

Munkar Nakeer Qabar Mein Humse Sawaal Jab Karein

Aisey Me Wird Hum Rakhein Aap Kay Naam Ka Faqat

Naza’a Kay Waqt Jab Meri Saanso(n) Mein Inteshaar Ho

Meri Nazar Ke Saamne Ghumbad Ho Wo Hara Faqat

Faqat Naat E Rasool  E Paak Hain Nazmi Ka Maqsad E Hayaat

Qabr Mein Bhi Labo(n) Pe Ho Sarkaar Ki Sana Faqat
