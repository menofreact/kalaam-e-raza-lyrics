Kushiyo ki bahaare laaya

Ramzan mubarak aaya…

dil joom ke mera gaaya

Ramzan mubarak aaya..

Qur’aa ki tilawat hogi

bhar pur ibaadat hogi

Quraan ka tohfa laaya

Ramzan mubarak aaya

sehri ki ajab raahat he

iftar me wo ronaq he

ik kaif ajab he chhaya

roze ki ye lazzat dekho

na pyas ki shiddat he wo

kya sabr he hum ne paaya

Ramzan to he oos ki

ho saath me maa jiski

”turkee” ne to ashk bahaya

jab Ramzan mubarak aaya

Allah meri maa ko

Ramzan ke sadqe me

sab maaf gunah karde

jannat bhi ataa karde

zahra ka mile saaya
