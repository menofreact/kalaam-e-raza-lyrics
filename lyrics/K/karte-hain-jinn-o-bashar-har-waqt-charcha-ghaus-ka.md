Karte Hain Jinn O Bashar Har Waqt Charcha Ghaus KaBaj Raha Hai Chaar Soo A’alam Mein Danka Ghaus Ka

Naar e Dozakh Se Bachayega Sahaara Ghaus KaLe Chalega Khuld Mein Adnaa Ishaara Ghaus Ka

Naz’a Mein Marqad Mein Mahshar Mein Madad FarmayengeHo Chuka Hai Ahad Pehle Hi Hamara Ghaus Ka

Khaaliq E Kaun O Makaan Ne Pehle Hi Roz E AzalLikh Diya Hai Meri Peshaani Pe Banda Ghaus Ka

Kya Ajab Be-Poochhe Mujh Ko Chorh Dein Munkar NakeerDekh Kar Mere Kafan Par Naam Likha Ghaus Ka

Naz’a Mein Marqad Mein Mahshar Mein Kahin Bhi Ya KhudaHaath Se Chhoote Na Daaman E Mu’alla Ghaus ka

Haa(n) Zaraa Thehro Firishto Phir Jo Chaaho PoochnaKar To Lene Do Mujhe Pehle Nazara Ghaus Ka

Sab Khaas O Khashaak Isyaa(n) Aan Mein Beh JaayegaJosh Par Aa Jaayega Jis Waqt Darya Ghaus Ka

Jholiyaa(n) Phailaao Daudo Bheek Lo Daaman BharoBat Raha Hai Aastaa(n) Par Aam Baada Ghaus Ka

Aankhein Malne Ke Liye Haath Aaye Chaukhat Ghaus KiSar Ragadne Ke Liye Mil Jaaye Rauza Ghaus Ka

Roshni E Sham’a Se Kya Kaam Andhi Aankh KoMo’mino(n) Ke Chashm E Dil Mein Hai Ujaala Ghaus Ka

Saltanat Shaah E Madina Ne Ataa Farmaayi HaiRaa-ij Iqleem E Wilaayat Mein Hai Sikka Ghaus Ka

Jalte Hain Dushman Khuda Ke Un Ke Zikr O Fikr SeWird Sab Allah Waale Karte Hain “Ya Ghaus” Ka

Kar Rahe Hain Ashqiya Koshish Ghataane Ki MagarRoz Afzoon Ho Raha Hai Bol Baala Ghaus Ka
