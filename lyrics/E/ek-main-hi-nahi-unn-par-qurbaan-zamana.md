Ek Main Hi Nahi Unn Par Qurbaan Zamana Hai

Jo Rabb E Do A’alam Ka Mehboob Yagana Hai

Kal Pul Se Hamey Jisne Khud Paar Lagana Hai

Zahra Ke Wo Baba Hai Hasnain Ke Nana Hai

Izzat Se Na Kyu Marr Jaayein Naam E Muhammad Par

Yun Bhi Kisi Din Humne Duniya Se To Jaana Hai

Pur Noor Siraahe Hai Ghumbad Pe Nigahein Hai

Jalwe Bhi Anokhe Hain Manzar Bhi Suhana Hai

Yeh Keh Ke Dar E Haq Se Li Maut Me Kuch Mohlat

Milaad Ki Aamad Hai Mehfil Ko Sajana Hai

Aao Dar E Zahra Par Failaaye Hue Daaman

Ye Nasl Kareemo Ki Lajpaal Gharana Hai

Mehroom E Karam Isko Rakhna Na Sar E Mehshar
