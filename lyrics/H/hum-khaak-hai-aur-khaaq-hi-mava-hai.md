Hum Khaak Hai Aur Khaaq Hi Mava Hai Hamara

Khaaki To Wo Aadam Jad E Aala Hai Hamara

ALLAH Humein Khaak Kare Apni Talab Me

Yeh Khaaq To Sarkaar Se Tamgha Hai Hamara

Jis Khaaq Pe Rakhte The Qadam Sayyide A’alam

Us Khaaq Pe Qurbaa(n) Dil E Shaida Hai Hamara

Kham Ho Gayi Pushte Falak Iss Ta’ane Zamee(n) Se

Sunn Hum Pe Madina Hai Wo Rutba Hai Hamara

Usne Laqab E Khaaq Shahenshah Se Paaya

Jo Haider E Karrar Ki Maula Hai Hamara

Ay Mudda’eyo! Khaak Ko Tum Khaak Na Samjhe

Iss Khaak Me Madfu(n) Shahe Bat-ha Hai Hamara

Hai Khaak Se Ta’ameer Mazaar E Shahe Qounain

Ma’mur Isi Khaak Se Kibla Hai Hamara

Hum Khaak Udayenge Jo Wo Khaak Na Paayi

AabadRazaJis Pe Madina Hai Hamara
