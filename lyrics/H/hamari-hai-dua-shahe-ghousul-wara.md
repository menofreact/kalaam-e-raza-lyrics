Hamari Hai Dua Shahe Ghousul Wara

Hara Bhara Rahe Ye Qadri Chaman

Main Qadri Hu Shukr Hai Rabbe Qadeer Ka

Hatho Mein Mere Haath Hai Peeran E Peer Ka

Imdad Kun Imdad Kun Az Bande Ghum Aazad Kun

Dar Deen O Duniya Shaad Kun Ya Ghous E Azam Dastagheer

Jiska Koyi Nahi Hai Sahara Uske Tum Ho Sahara

Kurba Jau Mein Ay Mere Meera Hain Kitna Pyara Sahara

Chalo Ay Aashiqo Chal Ke Ab Dekh Lo

Khila Hai Noor Se Ye Qadri Chaman

Tere Qadmo Mein Jo Maut Aaye Wo Maut Kitni Hasee(n) Ho

Baqsha Jaunga Rouz E Qayamat Itna Mujhko Yaqee(n) Hain

Tamanna Hai Meri Ho Chaadar Aapki

Khuda Kare Yahi Bane Mera Kafan
