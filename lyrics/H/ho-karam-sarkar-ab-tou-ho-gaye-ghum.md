Ho Karam Sarkar Ab Tou Ho Gaye Ghum Beshumaar

Jaan-O-Dil Tum Par Fida Ae Do Jahan Ke Taajdar

Mein Akela Aur Masail Zindagi Ke Beshumaar

Aap Hee Kuch Kijiye Na Ae Shah-e-Aali Waqaar

Yaad Aata Hai Tawaaf-e-Khana-e-Ka’aba Mujhe

Aur Lipatna Multazim Se Waalihana Baar Baar

Sung-e-Aswad Choom Kar Milta Mujhe Kaif-O-Suroor

Chayn Paata Dekh Kar Dil Mustajab-O-Mustajab

Gumbad-e-Khizra Ke Jalway Aur Woh Iftariyan

Yaad Aati Hain Bohot Ramzan-e-Taiba Ki Bahaar

Ya RasoolAllah Sunn Lejiye Meri Faryad Ko

Kon Hai Jou Keh Sunnay Tere Siwaa Meri Pukaar

Ghum Zadaa Yoon Na Huwa Hota Ubaid-e-Qadri

Iss Baras Bhi Dekhta Gar Sabz Gumbad Ki Bahar
