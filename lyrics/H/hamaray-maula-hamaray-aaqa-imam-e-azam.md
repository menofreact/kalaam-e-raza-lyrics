Hamaray Aaqa hamaray Moula Imam e Azam Abu Hanifa

Hamaray Muljaa hamaray Maa’wa Imam e Azam Abu Hanifa

Tum hi ko Maana, Tum hi ko Jana, Tumhi tou Sub k Imam Thehray

Mila na koi Imam Tum sa Imam e Azam Abu Hanifa

Zamanay bhar ne Zamanay bhar mein bohat tajassus kia hay lekin

Tumharay Aagay Tamam Aalam na kiun karay Zaano e Adab Kham

Kay Peshwayaan-e-Deen-e-Baala Imam e Azam Abu Hanifa

Na Kiun karain Naaz AhleSunnat kay Tum se Chamka Naseeb-e-Ummat

Siraj e Ummat Mila ho Tumsa Imam e Aazam Abu Hanifa

Hua Uloo al-Amr se ye Sabit kay teri Taa’at zaroor wajib

Khuda ne tum ko kia hamra Imam e Azam Abu Hanifa

Siraaj Tu hay baghair teray jo koi samajhe Hadees o Quraan

Phiray batakhta na paaey rasta Imam e Azam Abu Hanifa

Jo Teri Taqleed shirk hoti, Muhaddiseen Saaray hotay Mushrik

Bukhaari-o-Muslim Ibn Majah Imam e Azam Abu Hanifa

Ye jitnay Fuqaha Muhaddiseen hain, Tmharay Khirman se Khosha Cheen hain

wo Wastay se ye Bay Waseela Imam e Azam Abu Hanifa

Khabar le Ae Dastageer e Ummat Hay Saaiq e Be khabar pe Shiddat

Wo Tera ho kar phiray bathakta? Imame Azam Abu Hanifa
