Hum Dare Yaar Pe Dil Apna Jhuka Lete Hai

Sach Batana Ay Duniya Tera Kya Lete Hai

Pattharo Tum To Ho Patthar Aur Mere Aaqa

Tumse Gar Chahe To Kalmah Bhi Padha Lete Hai

Waqt Ki Qaid Nahi Hai Ye Karam Ki Baatein

Jab Bhi Sarkaar Ki Marzi Ho Bula Lete Hai

Usko Aankho Me Bithate Hai Zamane Wale

Jisko Mehboob e Khuda Apna Bana Lete Hai

Jab Nahi Milti Kahi Se Bhi Suku(n) Ki Daulat

Teri Mehfil Tere Deewane Saja Lete Hai

Jab Se Dekha HaiNiyaziWo Riyazul Jannah

Humto Ghar Baith Ke Jannat Ki Hawa Lete Hai
