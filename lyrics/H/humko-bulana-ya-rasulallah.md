Humko Bulana Ya RasulAllahHumko Bulana Ya HabibAllah

Kabhi To Sabz Gumbad Ka Ujala Hum Bhi DekhengeHamein Bulwaeinge Aaqa Madina Hum Bhi Dekhenge

Dhadak Uthhayga Ye Dil Ya Dharakna Bhool JayegaDil E Bismil Ka Uss Dar Par Tamasha Hum Bhi Dekhenge

Adab Sey Hath Bandhay Unkay Rozay Per Kharay HoungeSunehri Jaliyoon Ka Yun Nazara Hum Bhi Dekhenge

Dar E Doulat Sey Lootaya Nahin Jata Koi KhaliWahan Khayrat Ka Batna Khudaya Hum Bhi Dekhenge

Barasti Gumbad E Khazra Say Takrati Hoi BoundeinVahan Per Shaan Sey Baarish Barasna Hum Bhi Dekhenge

Guzaray Raat Din Apne Isi Umeed Per Hum NeKisi Din To Jamal E Ruey Zaiba Hum Bhi Dekhenge

Dam E Rukhsat Qadam Maan Bhar Kay Hein Mehsoos Kertay HainKisay Hai Jaakay Lout Aanay Ka Yara Hum Bhi Dekhenge

Puhanch Jayeinge Jis Din AyUjagarUnkay Qadmoon MainKisay Kehtay Hain Janat Ka Nazara Hum Bhi Dekhenge
