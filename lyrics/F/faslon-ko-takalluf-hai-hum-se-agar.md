Faslon Ko Takalluf Hai Hum Se Agar

Hum Bhi Bebas Nahin Be-sahara Nahi

Khud Unhi Ko Pukarengey Hum Duur Se

Raaste Mein Agar Paon Thak Jayengay

Hum Madine Mein Tanha Nikal Jayengey

Aur Galiyon Mein Kas Dan Bhatak Jayengey

Hum Wahan Jakey Wapas Nahin Ayengay

Dhoondhte Dhoondhte Loog Thak Jayengey

Naam E Aaqa Jahan Bhi Liya Jayega

Zikr Unka Jahan Bhi Kiya Jayega

Baat Barh Jayegi Dil Tarap Jayega
