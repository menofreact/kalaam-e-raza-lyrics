Dar E Sarkaar Pe Jaane Ki Dua Achhi Hai

Phir Waha Ja Ke Jo Aa Jaaye Qazaa Achhi Hai

Jis Zamee(n) Ne Tere Talwo Ke Liye Hai Bosey

Qasam ALLAH Ki Khaaq E Kaf E Paa Achhi Hai

Rouza E Paak E Payambar Se Jo Mass Ho Ke Chale

Rooh E Muztar Ke Liye Aisi Dawaa Achhi Hai

Isliye Malte They Zakhmo(n) Pe Lu-aab Hazrat Ka

Hai Sahaba Ka Aqeeda Ye Dawa Achhi Hai

Naam E Hazrat Pe Jo Sarr Apna Kataa Dete Hain

Jaan Lo Aysi Fanaa Mein Hi Baqaa Achhi Hai

Khud Khuda Aur Nabi Tujhse Hayaa Karte Hain

Mere Usmaan Teri Sharm O Hayaa Achhi Hai

Ibn Abbas Tere Rukh Ko Kabhi Maa Ko Takein

Bole Tere Rukh E Roushan Ki Ziyaa Achhi Hai

Kaash Mil Jaaye Madine Ka Mujhe Parwaana

Dil ESalmaanYahi Harf E Dua Achhi Hai
