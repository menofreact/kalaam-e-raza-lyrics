# Dar-e-nabi par yeh umr beetay ho ham pey lutf-e-dawaam aesa

**Categories:** D, Other Naat Shareef
**Source:** https://www.kalaam-e-raza.in/dar-e-nabi-par-yeh-umr-beetay-ho-ham/
**Lines:** 5

---

Yeh rif’at-e-zikr-e-mustafa hey naheen kisee ka maqaam aesaJo baad zikr-e-khuda hey afzal hey zikr-e-khair-ul-anaam aesa

Jo ghamzadon ko galay laga lay buron ko daman main jo chupa layHey dusra kaun is jahaan main siwa-e-khair-ul-anaam aesa

Bilaal tuj par nisaar jaoon kay khud nabi nain tujhe khareedanaseeb ho to naseeb aesa ghulam ho to ghulam aesa

Namaaz aqsa main jab parhaee to ambiya-o-rusool yeh bolayNamaaz ho to namaz aesee imam ho to imam aesa

Mein Khalid apnay nabi pey qurban hey jin ka khulq-e-azeem Qur’aanHey roshni jis kee do jahan main kaheen hey maah-e-tamaam aesa
