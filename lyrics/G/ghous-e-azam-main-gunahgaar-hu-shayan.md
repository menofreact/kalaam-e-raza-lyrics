Ghous E Azam Main Gunahgaar Hu Shay’An Lillah

Sakht Badkaaro Khatawaar Hu Shay’An Lillah

Aapke Hijr Ka Beemar Hu Shay’An Lillah

Taalib E Sharbat E Deedar Hu Shay’An Lillah

Mere Sultaan Main Nadaar Hu Shay’An Lillah

Be-kas O Be-bas O Laachaar Hu Shay’An Lillah

Ay Mere Chaand Ujala Ho Meri Jaanib Bhi

Sakht Zulmat Main Giraftaar Hu Shay’An Lillah

Aaj Kal Apne Pe Khud Baar Hu Shay’An Lillah

Aah Kis Darja Main Bekaar Hu Shay’An Lillah

Naqd Aamal Ki Punji Nahi Ratti Bhar Bhi

Phir Bhi Ye Za-am Ke Deendaar Hu Shay’An Lillah

Ek NazarGhaziE Be-kas Pe Ho Shah E Jeela(n)

Main Bhi Tera Sag E Darbaa Hu Shay’An Lillah
