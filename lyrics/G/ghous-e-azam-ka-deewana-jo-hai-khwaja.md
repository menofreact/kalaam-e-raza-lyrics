Ghous E Azam Ka Deewana Jo Hai Khwaja Ka Mastana

Jo Muhammad Pe Dil Se Fida Hain

Wo Bareiley Ka Ahmed Raza Hai

Jab Wo Paida Hua Noori Saaye Tale Baatil E Najd Mein Aa Gaye Zalzale

Kaise Uski Fazeelat Ka Suraj Dhale Jiski Azmat Ka Sikka Arab Me Chale

Jiska Khaaye Uska Gaaye Uska Naara Hum Lagaye, Jiska Naara Arab Mein Laga Hai

Jisne Ishq E Nabi Mein Utha Ke Qalam Mustafa Jaan E Rehmat Kiya Hai Raqam

Bad Aqeedoh Ka Jisne Giraya Alam Jisne Sarr Najdiyat Kiya Hai Qalam

Jab Bhi Ye Kisi Ne Poocha Har Taraf Se Naara Gunja, Ke Wo Khwaja Piya Ki Ataa Hai

Dasvi Tareekh Thi Maahe Shavval Tha Ghar Naqi Khan Ke Beta Paida Hua

Naam Ahmad Raza Uska Rakhha Gaya Aala Hazrat Zamane Ka Wo Bann Gaya

Ilm O Hiqmat Ka Khazana Ho Mujaddid E Zamana Najdiyo Se Jo Tanha Lada Hai

Jab Raza Kha(n) Ko Bay’at Ki Khwaish Hui Pohache Marehra Wo Umr Baayis Ki Thi

Faiz E Barkaat Se Aysi Barkat Mili Saath Bay’at Ke Fauran Khilafat Mili

Aala Hazrat Unko Tanha Sirf Main Hi Nahi Kehta Achhe Achho Ko Kehna Pada Hai
