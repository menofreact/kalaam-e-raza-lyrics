Rusul Unhi Ka To Muzdah sunane Aaye Hai

Unhi Ke Aane Ki Khushiya Manane Aaye Hai

Firishte Aaj Jo Dhume Machane Aaye Hai

Unhi Ke Aane Ki Shadi Rachane Aaye Hai

Falak Ke Hur O Malak Geet Gaane Aaye Hai

Ki Do Jahan Me Ye Danke Bajane Aaye Hai

Ye Bhoole Bichdo Ko Raste Pe Laane Aaye Hai

Ye Bhoole Bhatko Ko Hadi Banane Aaye Hai

Chamak Se Apni Jahan Jagmagane Aaye Hai

Mahak Se Apni Ye Kunche Basane Aaye Hai

Naseem E Faiz Se Gunche Khilane Aaye Hai

Karam Ki Apni Bahaare Dikhane Aaye Hai

Yahi To Sote Huwo Ko Jagane Aaye Hai

Yahi To Rote Huwo Ko Hasane Aaye Hai

Unhe Khuda Ne Kiya Apne Milk Ka Maalik

Unhi Ke Qabze Me Rabb Ke Khazane Aaye Hai

Jo Chahenge Jise Chahenge Ye Use Denge

Kareem Hai Ye Khazane Lutane Aaye Hai
