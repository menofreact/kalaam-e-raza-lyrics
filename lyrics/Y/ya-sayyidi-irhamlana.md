Ya Syayidi Irhamlana Ya Syedi Irhamlana

O Master, be merciful towards us,O Master, be merciful towards usO Master, be merciful towards us,O Master, be merciful towards us

Innil Tiya ree Hassaba Youmun Ilaa Ardil HaramBalligh Salaami Roudatun Feehannabiyul MohtaramO Winds! If someday you pass by the sacred land,Do give my greetings (salaam) at the Blessed Grave where the most exalted Prophet rests

Maw Wajhohu Shams-ud-Duha Mun Khaddohu Badr-ud-DujaMaw Zaatohu Noor-ul-Huda Mun Kaffohu Bahr-ul-HemamHe whose blessed face is the midday sun, his blessed cheeks are the full moon,He whose self is the light of guidance and whose generosity is an ocean

The Qur’an is our source proving other religions are abrogated,When its guidelines came to us, the other books became non-existent

Tooba Le-Ahle Baldatin Fee-hun-Nabiyul Mohtasham

The sadness of separation [from Madinah] feels like a sword deep into my liver,How fortunate are the people of that city in which the Exalted & Beloved Prophet resides

O Mercy for the worlds! You are the intercessor for the sinners,Shower your blessings upon us on the Day of Grief

O Mercy for the worlds! Assist Zaynul Abideen,Who stands alone and in difficulty amongst the tyrants.
