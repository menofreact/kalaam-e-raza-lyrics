# Jalwa Hai Noor Hai Ke Sarapa Raza Ka Hai

**Categories:** J, Manqabat
**Source:** https://www.kalaam-e-raza.in/jalwa-hai-noor-hai-ke-sarapa-raza-ka/
**Lines:** 9

---

Dastaar Arahi Hai Zameen Par Jo Sar UtheItna Buland Aaj Harayra Raza Ka Hai

Kis Ki Majaal Hai Jo Nazar Bhi Mila SakayDarbar-e-Mustafa sallallahu alaihe wasallam Mein Thikana Raza Ka Hai

Alfaz Beh Rahay Hain Daleelon Ki Dhaar ParChalta Hua Qalam Hai Ke Dhara Raza Ka Hai

Choota Hai Asman Ko Minaar Azm KaYaani Atal Pahad Irada Raza Ka Hai

Nuktay Ibaratoun Se Ubhartay Hai Khud Ba KhudNakhdo Nazar Pe Aisa Ijara Raza Ka Hai

Darya Fasahatoun Ke Rawan Shayri Mein HaiYeh Sehl-e-Mumtanay Hai Ke Lehja Raza Ka Hai

Jo Likh Diya Hai Usne Sanad Hai Deen Woh Deen MeinAhle Qalam Ki Abru Nukhta Raza Ka Hai

Agloun Ne Agla Bhi Likha Bohat Ilm-e-Deen ParPar Jo Kuch Hai Is Sadi Mein Woh Tanha Raza Ka Hai

Is Dour-e-Pur Fitan Nazar Khush AqeedgiSarkar sallallahu alaihe wasallam Ka Karam Hai Waseela Raza Ka Hai
