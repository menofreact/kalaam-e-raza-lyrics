Bigri Bhe Banain Ge Jalwy Bhe Dekhain ge

Ghabrao Na Deewano Sarkar Bolain Ge

Hum masjid e Nabvi key Dekhy Ge Minaroon Ko

Aur gumbad E Khazran key Purnoor nazaroon Ko

Hum Ja Key Madina Phir Wapis Nahi Aay ge

Mil Jay Ge Tabirein Eik Rooz To khawaboo Ki

Gir Jay Gein Dewarein Sab Dekhna Rahoo Ki

Hum Roza E Aqdas Par Jab Ansoo Bahain Ge

Dil Ishq E Nabi mey Tum Kuch Aur Tarapny Doo

Is Deed Ki Atish Ko Kuch Aur Bharkny Doo

Hum Tishna Dily Chal Kar Zam Zam Sey Bojhay Ge

Jab Hashar Key Medan Mey Eik Hashar Bapaa Ho Ga

Jab Fesla Ummat Ka Karny Ko Khuda Ho Ga

Ummat Ko Shahe batah Kamli Mey Chupain ge

Lillah Mohammad Sey Rodad Meri kehna

Ye Poch Key Aaqa Sey Ey Hajion Tum Ana
