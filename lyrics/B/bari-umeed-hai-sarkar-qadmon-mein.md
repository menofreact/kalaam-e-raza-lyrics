# Bari umeed hai Sarkar qadmon mein bulayenge

**Categories:** B, Other Naat Shareef
**Source:** https://www.kalaam-e-raza.in/bari-umeed-hai-sarkar-qadmon-mein/
**Lines:** 5

---

Agar jaana Madine mein huwa hum gham ke maaron kamakeene Gumbad-e-Khazra ko haal-e-dil sunayenge

Kasam Allah ki ho ga woh manzar deed ke qabilQayamat mein Rasulallah jab tashreef layenge

Qayamat tak jagayenge na phir Munkar Nakeer uskoLahad mein wo jisse apna Rukh-e-Zaiba dikhayenge

Gunahgaron mein khud aa aa kar shamil parsaa hongeShafi-e-Hashr jab Daaman-e-Rehmat mein chupayenge

Gham-e-Ishq-e-Nabi se hoga jab maamoor dil nayyarTere zulmat kade mein bhi sitare jagmagayenge
