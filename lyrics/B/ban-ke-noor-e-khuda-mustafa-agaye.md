Ban ke Noor e Khuda, Mustafa agaye

Marhaba Marhaba Mustafa agaye

Ujri dunya thi Aaqa ne aabaad ki

Har taraf dhuum Unke he Milaad ki

Sab ke Mushkil kusha, Mustafa agaye

Ho gaye Noor dunya ke zulmat talle

Jin ke aane se Mao ko bete mille

Sarwar-e-Ambiyaa, Mustafa agaye

Arsh par jashan Jinn ka manaya gaya

Sab Rasulon ko jiss me bulaaya gaya

Haa wohi Dil Ruba Mustafa agaye

Ab na maa’yuus hoga sawaali koi

Aaj Naasir na lote ga khaali koi

Muskura e gada , Mustafa agaye
