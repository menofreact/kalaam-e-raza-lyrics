# bahar-e jaan(n) fiza tum ho, nasim-e gulsita(n) tum ho

**Categories:** B, Other Naat Shareef
**Source:** https://www.kalaam-e-raza.in/bahar-e-jaann-fiza-tum-ho-nasim-e/
**Lines:** 32

---

bahar-e jaan(n) fiza tum ho, nasim-e gulsita(n) tum hobahar-e bagh-e rizwan tum se hai, zaibe jinan tum hokhuda ki saltanut ka do-jahan main kaun dulha hai,tumhee, tum ho, tumhee, tum ho, yahan tum ho, wahan tum hohaqeeqat se tumhari juz khuda aur kaun waqif hai,kahe tu kya kahe koyee, chuneen tum ho, chunaan tum honazar ‘arif ka har ‘alam main aya aap ka ‘alam,na hote tum tu kya hota, bahar har jahan tum hokuju hum khaak uftadah, kuja tum ae shah-e bala,agar misl-e zamin hum hain, tu misl-e asman tum homain bhula aap kī rif’at se nisbat hee hamein kya hai,woh kehne bhar kee nisbat thee, kahan hum hain, kahan tum hohamein umeed hai rauz-e qiyamat unkee rehmat se,ke farmayein idhar a’ao na-mayus az-jinan tum hositam karo, khata karo, siyah karo, jafa karo,hamare daman-e rehmat main aa jaoo kahna tum hotumhare hote saate dard dukh kis se kahoon pyare,shafī’e asiyan tum ho, waqīl-e mujriman tum horiyazat ke yahee din hain burhape main kahan himmat,jo kuch karna hai abhi karlo, abhi noori jawan tum hofaqat nisbat ka jaise hoon haqiqinooriho jau,mujhe jo dekhe keh uthe miyan noori, miyan tum ho
