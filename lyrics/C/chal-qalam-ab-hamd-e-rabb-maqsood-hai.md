Chal Qalam Ab Hamd E Rabb Maqsood Hai

Tera Mera Sab Ka Jo Ma’bood Hai

Hai Wahi Shahid Wahi Mash-hud Hai

Noor Uska Har Jagah Maujood hai

Usne Hi Humko Bakshe Hay Humko Mustafa

Ye Na Ho To Zindagi Be Sud Hai

Yu To Hai Quraa(n) Hidayat Ki Kitaab

Par Kisi Ka Tazkera Maksood Hai

Wad-duha Wash-shams Jiski Shaan Hai

Haan Wahi Ahmed Wahi Mehmood Hai

Unn Ka Charcha Har Jabaa(n) Par Hai Rawaa(n)

Unki Khushbu Har Jagah Maujood Hai

Azmat E Ahmad Me Jisko Hai Shuba

Haan Wahi Shaitaa(n) Wahi Mardood Hai

Aur Jo Karta Hai Unn Par Jaa(n) Fida

Wo Mubarak Hai Wahi Mas-ud Hai

Noor Ki Barsaat Hoti Hai Waha

Jis Jagah Par Mehfil E Maulood Hai

Padhte RahiyeNazmiNaat E Mustafa

Haan Isi Me Rooh Ki Beh-bud Hai
