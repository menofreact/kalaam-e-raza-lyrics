# Qadam qadam pe Khuda ki madad pohanchti hai

**Categories:** Other Naat Shareef, Q
**Source:** https://www.kalaam-e-raza.in/qadam-qadam-pe-khuda-ki-madad-pohanchti/
**Lines:** 7

---

qadam qadam pe Khuda ki madad pohanchti haidurood se mere dil ko rasad pohanchti haiye aasmaan bhi wahan tak nahi pohanch saktajahan tak aap k qadmon ki hadd pohanchti haisir apna paaye risalat ma’aab per rakh doonto aasmaan pe bulandi-e-qadd pohanchti haijuloos-e-ishq-e-nabi ka ho jis taraf se guzarmere junoon ko bhi lay kar khirad pohanchti haidurood ka dia jata hai jub sawab mujhebahisht tak meri do guz lahad pohanchti haisalaam-o-naat muzaffar yahan mein parhta hoonqubooliat ki haram se sanad pohanchti hai.

qadam qadam pe Khuda ki madad pohanchti haidurood se mere dil ko rasad pohanchti hai

ye aasmaan bhi wahan tak nahi pohanch saktajahan tak aap k qadmon ki hadd pohanchti hai

sir apna paaye risalat ma’aab per rakh doonto aasmaan pe bulandi-e-qadd pohanchti hai

juloos-e-ishq-e-nabi ka ho jis taraf se guzarmere junoon ko bhi lay kar khirad pohanchti hai

durood ka dia jata hai jub sawab mujhebahisht tak meri do guz lahad pohanchti hai

salaam-o-naat muzaffar yahan mein parhta hoonqubooliat ki haram se sanad pohanchti hai.
