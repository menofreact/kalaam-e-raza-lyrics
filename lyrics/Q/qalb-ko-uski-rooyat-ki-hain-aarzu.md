Qalb Ko Uski Rooyat Ki Hain Aarzu

Jiska Jalwa Hain Aalam Me Har Chaar Su

Balki Khud Nafs Me Hain Wo Subhanuhu

Asrh Par Hai Magar Arsh Ko Justuju

ALLAHU ALLAHU ALAHU ALLAHU

Arsh Farsh Wa Zamaan Wa Jehat Ay Khuda

Jis Taraf Dekhta Hu Hai Jalwa Tera

Zarre Zarre Ki Aankhon Me Tu Hi Ziya

Qatre Qatre Ki Tu Hi To Hain Aabaru

Tu Kisi Jaa Nahi Aur Har Jaa Hai Tu

Tu Munazza Makaa Se Mubarrah Jasu

Ilm Wa Qudrat Se Har Ja Hai Tu Ku-ba-ku

Tere Jalwe Hai Har Har Jagah Ay Afu

Tayerane Janaa Me Teri Guftugu

Geet Tere Hi Gaate Hain Wo Khush Gulu

Koi Kehta Hai Haq Koi Kehta Hai Hu

Aur Sab Kehte Hain La Sharikala Hu

Raham Farma Khudaya Haram Paak Ho

Tune Taqdees Bakshi Hai Jis Khaaq Ko

Daf’a FarmaWahaa Par Hai Be Baak Ju

Aur Gira Bijliyan Kahar Ki Bar Adu

Khwabe Noori Me Aaye Jo Noore Khuda

Bukkae Noor Ho Apna Zulmat Qada

Jagmaga Uththe Dil Chehra Ho Pur  Ziya

Nooriyo Ki Tarah Shagl Ho Zikre Hu
