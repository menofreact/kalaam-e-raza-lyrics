Sarwar Kahoon Kay Maalik-o-Maula Kahoon Tujhay

Baagh-e-Khaleel Ka Gul-e-Zaiba Kahoon Tujhay

Subh-e-Watan Pay Shaam-e-Ghareeban Ko Doon Sharaf

Bey-Kus Nawaaz Gey-Sue-Oon Wala Kahoon Tujhay

Allah Nay Teyray Jism-e-Munawwar Kee Taa-Bish-Aeyn

Aey Jaan-e-Jaan Mein Jaan-e-Tajalla Kahoon Tujhay

Mujrim Hoon Apnay Af’oo Ka Samaan Karoon Shah-aa

Ya’nee Shafe’e Roz-e-Jaza Ka Kahoon Tujhay

Teyray Tou Wasf A’eb Ta-Na-Hee Say Hain Baree

Hairaan Hoon Meyray Shah Mein Kya Kya Kahoon Tujhay

Keh Lay Gee Sub Kuch Unkay Sana Khuwaan Kee Khaam-Shee

Chup Ho Raha Hoon Keh Kay Mein Kiya Kiya Kahoon Tujhay

Leykin Raza Nay Khatm-e-Sukhan Iss Pay Kar Dia

Khaaliq Ka Banda Khalq Ka Aaqa Kahoon Tujhay
