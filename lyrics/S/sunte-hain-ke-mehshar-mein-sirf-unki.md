Sunte Hain Ke Mehshar Mein Sirf Unki Rasaai Hai

Gar Unki Rasaai Hai Lo Jab To Ban Aayi Hai

Machla Hai Ke Rehmat Ne Ummeed Bandhaai Hai

Kya Baat Teri Mujrim Kya Baat Banaai Hai

Sab Ne Saf E Mehshar Mein Lalkaar Diya Hum Ko

Aye Bekaso(N) Ke Aaqa Ab Teri Duhaai Hai

Yun To Sab Unhi Ka Hai Par Dil Ki Agar Poocho

Yeh Toote Hue Dil Hi Khaas Unki Kamaai Hai

Zaair Gaye Bhi Kab Ke Din Dhalne Pe Hai Pyaare

Uth Mere Akele Chal Kya Der Lagaai Hai

Baazar E Amal Mein Toh Sauda Na Bana Apna

Sarkar Karam Tujh Mein Aibee Ki Samaai Hai

Girte Huwo Ko Muzda Sajde Mein Gire Maula

Ro Ro Ke Shafa’at Ki Tamheed Uthaai Hai

Aye Dil Yeh Sulagna Kya Jalna Hai To Jal Bhi Uth

Dum Ghutne Laga Zaalim Kya Dhooni Ramaai Hai

Mujrim Ko Na Sharmao Ahbaab Kafan Dhak Do

Munh Dekh Ke Kya Hoga Parde Mein Bhalaai Hai

Ab Aap Hi Sambhalein Toh Kaam Apne Sambhal Jaayein

Humne To Kamai Sab Khelon Mein Ganwaai Hai

Aye Ishq Tere Sadqe Jalne Se Chhute Saste

Joh Aag Bujha Degi Woh Aag Lagaai Hai

Hirso Hawas E Bad Se Dil Tu Bhi Sitam Kar Le

Tu Hi Nahi Beygana Dunya Hi Paraai Hai

Hum Dil Jale Hain Kiske Hat Fitnon Ke Parkaale

Kyun Phoonk Du Ik Ufs Se Kya Aag Lagaai Hai

Taiba Na Sahi Afzal Makkah Hi Bada Zaahid

Hum Ishq Ke Bande Hain Kyun Baat Badhaai Hai

Matl’A Mein Yeh Shak Kya Tha WallahRazaWallah

Sirf Unki Rasaai Hai Sirf Unki Rasaai Hai
