Maulood-E-Mustafa Hai Ye Jashn Ka Sama Hai  Ye Subh-E-Baharaan Hai Ye Jashn-E-Mustafa Hai

Subh-E-Baharaan Aayei Hai Jhoomo  Jashn-E-Baharaan Milkay Manao  Shaadmaani Ke Taranay Goonjtay Hain Har Jagah  Aashiqaan-E-Mustafa Jhoomtay Hain Har Jagah  Subh-E-Baharaan Aayei Hai Jhoomo  Jashn-E-Baharaan Milkay Manao

Deep Khushi Ke Milkay Jalao  Aaye Hain Aaqa Sabko Batao

Jashn-E-Nabi Phir Jashn-E-Nabi Hai  Karlo Charaghaan Rung Jamao

Bachon Ko Apne Dars Ye Dena  Aal-E-Nabi Ka Ishq Sikhao

Roothay Hain Jou Bhi Unko Manao  Rung-E-Mohabbat Dil Pe Charhao

Deen-E-Nabi Ke Tum Ho Muhafiz  Apni Jawani Isspe Lutao

Chahtay Ho Gar Sheyron Ka Charcha  Rung Raza Ka Naat Mein Lao

Rehmat-E-Rab Hai Aamad-E-Aaqa  Ne’matein Rab Ki Khushiyan Manao

Dil Ki Laggi Tum Aur Barhao  Naat Ujagar Aur Sunao
