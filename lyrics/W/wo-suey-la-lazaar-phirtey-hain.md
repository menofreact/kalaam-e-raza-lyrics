Wo Suey La Lazaar Phirte Hain

Tere Din Ay Bahaar Phirte Hain

Jo Tere Dar Se Yaar Phirte Hain

Dar-Ba-Dar Yu Hi Khaar Phirte Hain

Phool Kya Dekhu Meri Aankhon Mein

Dashte Taibah Ke Khaar Phirte Hain

Jaan Hain Jaan Kya Nazar Aaye,

Kyu Adu Gird E Gaar Phirte Hain

Unke Ima Pe Dono Bagho Par,

Khaile Lailo Nahar Phirte Hain

Wardiya Bolte Hain Har Kaare

Pehra Dete Suvaar Phirte Hain

Laakhon Qudsi Hain Kaam-E-Khidmat Par

Laakhon Girde Mazaar Phirte Hain

Har Charagh E Mazaar Par Qudsi

Kaise Parwana Vaar Phirte Hain

Baaye Raste Na Ja Musafir Sunn

Maal Hain Rahbaar Phirte Hain

Jaag Sunsaan Ban Hai Raat Aayi

Gurg Behre Shikaar Phirte Hain

Rakhiye Jaise Hain Khanazad Hain Hum

Mol Ke Aibdaar Phirte Hain

Us Gali Ka Gada Hu Main Jisme

Maangte Taajdaar Phirte Hain

Haye Gafil Wo Kya Jagah Hain Jaha

Paanch Jaate Hain Chaar Phirte Hain

Koyi Kyu Pooche Teri BaatRaza

Tujhse Kutte Hazaar Phirte Hain
