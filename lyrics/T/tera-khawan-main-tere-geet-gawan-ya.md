Tera khawan main Tere geet gawan, Ya RasoolallahTera milaad mein kyon na manawaan, Ya Rasolallah.

Halima kar qadi vekhay, kadi Sarkaar (SAW) nu wakhay,Main keri sayj Tere lai sajawan, Ya Rasoolallah.

Mera dil vi aie chahnda aey, Tusi merey we ghar aao,Par mein kerey maan thi tainu bulaanwaan, Ya Rasoolallah.

Jagaao bhaag merey vi Abu Ayyub dey vangoon,Muqaddar os da kithon liyawan, Ya Rasoolallah.

Mayn kujh vi nai jay Teray naal meri koi nisbat nahin,Mayn sab kuch haan je mayn Tera sadaawan, Ya Rasoolallah

Suna hai Aap har ashiq kay ghar tashreef latay hain,Meray ghar mein bhi ho jaye charaghan, Ya Rasulallah.

Madinay paak day under meri ayho eibadar hai,Teray rozay toon na nazran hatawaan, Ya Rasulallah.
