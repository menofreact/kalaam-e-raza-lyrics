Tumhaare Dar Pe Jo Main Baryaab Ho Jaau

Qasam Khuda Ki Shaha Kaamyaab Ho Jaau

Jo Paau Bosa E Paa E Huzoor Kya Kehna

Main Zarra Shams O Qamar Ka Jawab Ho Jaau

Meri Haqeeqat E Faani Bhi Kuch Haqeeqat Hai

Maru(n) To Aaj Khayal Aur Khwaab Ho Jaau

Jahaan Ke Qaus O Qaza Se Farayb Khaye Kyun

Main Apne Qalb O Nazar Ka Hijaab Ho Jaau

Jahaan Ki Bigdi Isi Aastane Se Banti Hai

Main Kyu Na Waqf Dar E AanJanaab Ho Jaau

Tumhara Naam Liya Hai Talatam E Ghum Mein

Main Ab To Paar Risaalat Ma-aab Ho Jaau

Ye Meri Doori Badal Jaaye Qurb SeAkhtar

Agar Woh Chahein To Mein Baryaab Ho Jaau
