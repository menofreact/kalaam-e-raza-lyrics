# Tum Chalo Hum Chalein Sab Madine Chalein

**Categories:** Other Naat Shareef, T
**Source:** https://www.kalaam-e-raza.in/tum-chalo-hum-chalein-sab-madine-chalein/
**Lines:** 18

---

Tum Chalo Hum Chalein Sab Madine Chalein

Jaanib E Taybah Sab Ke Safeene Chalein

Maikasho! Aao Aao Madine Chalein

Baada E Khuld Ke Jaam Peene Chalein

Jee Gaye Wo Madine Mein Jo Marr Gaye

Aao Hum Bhi Wahaa(N) Mar Ke Jeene Chalein

Taahir E Jaa(N) Madine Ko Jab Udd Chalaa

Zindagi Se Kaha Zindagi Ne Chalein

Raah E Taybah Mein Jab Naatwaa(N) Reh Gaye

Dil Ko Kheenche Kaha Be Kali Ne Chalein

Khaaq E Taybah Mein Apni Jagah Ho Gayi

Khoob Muzda Sunaaya Khushi Ne Chalein

Agley Pichley Sabhi Khuld Mein Chal Diye

Rouz E Mehshar Kahaa Jab Nabi Ne Chalein

Un Ki Shaan E Karam Ki Kashish Dekhna

Kaasa Lekar Kahaa Khusrawi Ne Chalein

AkhtarE Khasta Bhi Khuld Mein Chal Diya

Jab Sadaa Di Usey Murshidi Ne Chalein
