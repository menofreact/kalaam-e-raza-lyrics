Naat Sarkar Ki Parta Hoon Mein

Bas Issi Baat Say Ghar Mein Meri Rahmat Hogi

Ik Tera Naam Waseela He Mera

Ranj O Gham Mey Bhi Iss Naam Sey Rahat Hogi

Ye Suna Hey Kay Bahot Ghor Andheri Hogi

Qabar Ka Khowf Na Rakna Ey Dil

Waha Sarkar E Madina Ki Ziarat Hogi

Unko Mukhtar Banaya Hey Merey Mola Ney

Khuld Mein Bas Wahi Ja Sakta Hein

Jisko Hasnain Kay Baba Ki Ijazat Hogi

Unki Chokat Pey Parey He to Bari Moj Mein Hey

Lot Kay Ayengey Jab Us Dar Sey

Merey Dil Tu Hi Bata Kya Teri Halat Hogi

Hashr Ka Din Bhi Ajab Deykna Vala Hoga

Zulf Lehra Key Wo Jab Ayengey

Phir Qiyamat Mein Bhi Ik Aur Qiayamat Hogi

Rashk Sey Door Karey Deyktey Hogay Zaahid

Jab Sare Hashr Gunahgaro Pey

Merey Aaqa Ka Karam Hoga Inayat Hogi

Kahi Ta Ha Kahi Yaseen Kahi Washams Aaya

Jiski Qasmay Mera Rab Kaata Hey

Kitni Dil Kash Merey Mehboob Ki Surat Hogi

Do Jahaan Mein Usey Kon Pana Deyga

Hoga Ruswa Sare Hashr Jise

Syeda Zahra Kay Bach Sey Adawat Hogi

Mayray Sarkaar Bulaa Ain Gay, Un Ki Chokat Pay,

Wo Nazaara Phir May Daykoon Ga,

Daykh E Kar Gumbad E Khazra, Mayri Ulfat Hogi

Mera Daman to Gunaho Sey Bhara Hey Iltaf

Ik Sahara Hey Kay Mein Unka Hoon

Issi Nisbat Sey Sar-e-hashr Shifa-at Hogi
