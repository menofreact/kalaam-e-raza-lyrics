# Ay Habib E Ahmed E Mujtaba Dil E Mubtala Ka Salam Lo

**Categories:** A, Salaam
**Source:** https://www.kalaam-e-raza.in/ay-habib-e-ahmed-e-mujtaba-dil-e/
**Lines:** 3

---

Mai Talab Se Baaz Na Aaunga Tu Karam Ka Haath Badhaye JaJo Tere Karam Se Hai Aashna Usi Aashna KaSalam Lo

Koi Mar Raha Hai Bahisht Par Koi Chahata Hai Najaat koMai Tujhi Ko Chahu Khuda Kare Meri Is Duaa kaSalam Lo

Tamam Auliya Ke Buland Sar Hai Qadam Pe Jinke Jhuke HuveUsi Pyare Ghous Ka Wasta Ke Har Ik Gada KaSalam Lo
