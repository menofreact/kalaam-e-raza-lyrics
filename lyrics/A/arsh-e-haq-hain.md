Arshe Haq He Musnade Rif’at Rasoolullah ﷺ Ki

Dekhni He Hashr Me Izzat Rasoolullah ﷺ Ki

Qabr Me Lehrayenge Tha Hasr Chashme Noor Ke

Jalwah Farma Hogi Jab Tal’at Rasoolullah ﷺ Ki

Kaafiro Par Taighe Waala Se Giri Bakre Ghazab

Abr-Aasa Cha Gayi Haibat Rasoolullah ﷺ Ki

Laa Wa Rabbil Arsh Jisko Jo Mila Unse Mila

Bat’ti He Kownain Me Ne’mat Rasoolullah ﷺ Ki

Wo Jahannum Me Gaya Jo Unse Mustaghni Huwa

Hai Khalleelullah Ko Haajat Rasoolullah ﷺ Ki

Sooraj Ulte Paaw Palte Chaand Ishaare Se Ho Chaak

Andhe Najdi Dekhle Qudrat Rasoolullah ﷺ Ki

Tujhse Aur Jannat Se Kya Matlab Wahabi Duur Ho

Hum Rasoolullah Ke, Jannat Rasoolullah ﷺ Ki

Zikr Roke Fazal Kaate Naqs Ka Juya(N) Rahe

Phir Kahe Mardak Ke Hu Ummat Rasoolullah ﷺ Ki

Najdi Usne Tujhko Mohlat Di Ki Iss Aalam Me Hai

Kaafiro Murtad Pe Rehmat Rasoolullah ﷺ Ki

Hum Bhikaari Wo Kareem, Unka Khudaa Unse Fazoo

Aur Naa Kehna Nahee Aadat Rasoolullah ﷺ Ki

Ahle Sunnat Ka He Bera Paar Ashaabe Rasool

Najm He Aur Naav Hai Itrat Rasoolullah ﷺ Ki

Khaaq Ho Kar Ishq Me Aaram Se Sona Mila

Jaan Ki Akseer Hai Ulfat Rasoolullah ﷺ Ki

Toot Jaa-Enge Gunaahgaro Ke Fawran Qaid-O-Bund

Hashr Ko Khuljaaegi Taaqat Rasoolullah ﷺ Ki

Ya Rabb Ik Saa’at Me Dhul Jaa-E Siyaahkaro Ke Jurm

Josh Par Aa Jaaye Ab Rehmat Rasoolullah ﷺ Ki

Hai Ghule Baaghe Qudus Rukhsaar E Zeba E Huzoor

Sarve Ghulzaar E Qadam Kaamat Rasoolullah ﷺ Ki

AeRazaKhud Saahibe Quraan Hai Mud’daahe Huzoor

Tujhse Kab Mumkin He Phir Midhat Rasoolullah ﷺ Ki
