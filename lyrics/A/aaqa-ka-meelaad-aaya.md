Ya RasoolAllah Ya HabibAllahSalla Alaika Ya RasoolAllah

Wassallam Alaika Ya HabibAllah

Ahlaon Wa Sahlan Marhaba Ya RasoolAllah

Charoon Taraf Noor Chahya Aaqa Ka Milad AayaKhushiyon Ka Pegham Laya Aaqa Ka Milad Aaya

Shams-O-Qamar Aur Taaray Kyun Na Ho Khush Aaj Saaray

Unse Hee Tou Noor Paaya Aaqa Ka Milad Aaya

Khushiyan Manatay Hain Wohi Dhoomein Machatay Hain WohiJinn Per Hua Unka Saaya Aaqa Ka Milad Aaya

Hai Shaad Har Ek Musalmaan Karta Hai Ghar Ghar Charaghaan

Galiyun Ko Bhi Jagmagaya Aaqa Ka Milad Aaya

Mukhtar-e-Kul Maanay Jou Unhe Noori Bashar Jaanay Jou UnheNa’ara Ussi Ne Lagaya Aaqa Ka Milad Aaya

Jou Aaj Mehfil Mein Aaye Mun Ki Muradein Woh Paaye

Sab Par Karam Ho Khudaya Aaqa Ka Milad Aaya

Ghous Ul Wara Aur Data Ne Mere Raza Aur Khuwaja NeSab Ne Hee Din Ye Manaya Aaqa Ka Milad Aaya

Na’at-e-Nabi Tum Sunao Ishq-e-Nabi Ko Barhao

Hum Ko Raza Ne Sikhaya Aaqa Ka Milad Aaya

Jiss Ko Shajar Jaantay Hain Kehna Hajar Maantay HainAisa Nabi Hum Ne Paaya Aaqa Ka Milad Aaya

Dil Jagmaganay Lagay Hain Sab Muskaranay Lagay Hain

Ik Kaif Sa Aaj Chahya Aaqa Ka Milad Aaya

Kar Ae Ubaid Unki Midhat Tujh Per Khuda Ki Ho RehmatTu Ne Muqqadar Ye Paaya Aaqa Ka Milad Aaya
