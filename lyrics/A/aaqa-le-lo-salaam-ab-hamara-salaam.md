# Aaqa Le Lo Salaam Ab Hamara (Salaam)

**Categories:** A, Salaam
**Source:** https://www.kalaam-e-raza.in/aaqa-le-lo-salaam-ab-hamara-salaam/
**Lines:** 4

---

Ghum se Hain Toote Hue, Zulmo Se Loote Hue,Muddat Hui Ya Nabi, Qismat Ko Roothe Hue,Rouze Mehshar Ummati Ka Aap Hi Sahara

Din Raat Rooti Hain Ye, Ummat Tumhare LiyeHo Jaye Nazre Karam, Aaqa Hamare LiyeTaibah Ki Un Galiyon Ka Kab Hoga Nazara

Sab Ambiya Ke Imam, Tum Par Ho Laakho SalaamRouze Pe Aaye Majeed, Kehta Hai Ye Subho ShaamZindagi Me Aas Ka Na Toote Sitara

Aaqa Le Lo Salaam Ab Hamara
